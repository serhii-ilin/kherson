# Fortress and old town / Фортеця, старе місто

Research brief for the hero models of the old town (realistic-buildings plan, [BUILDINGS_PLAN.md](../BUILDINGS_PLAN.md)). Target state: as photographed in 2016–2021, before 24 February 2022. Footprints and rotations come from the OSM snapshot. Dimensions not stated in a source are marked *estimate*, and are checked against the photos before a model is accepted.

## Per-building notes

Each building gets a section here before its hero task starts: massing (storeys, heights from photos), bay rhythm per facade, openings, orders and mouldings, roof and domes, colours (hex) and PBR layers, and the photo faces chosen as textures, with crop corners.
